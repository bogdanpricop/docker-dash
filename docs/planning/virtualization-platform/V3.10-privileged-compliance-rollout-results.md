# V3.10 — Privileged access and compliance rollout results

## Scope

Release v8.84.0 is the exact B169–B178 batch. B169–B175 receive a new
privileged-access and compliance control plane. B176–B178 were already `Done`
and are revalidated as the permission-catalog, custom-role and scope-hierarchy
foundation used by the new controls; they are not claimed as newly delivered.

## Current result

- local TOTP step-up is rate-limited and produces a TTL-bound pending JIT
  request;
- independent typed approval precedes a requester-only one-time token claim,
  and only token hashes are stored;
- break-glass requires ticket, notification references, four eyes, one-time
  activation, expiry/closure and independent review;
- break-glass remains a scoped authorization envelope, not a standalone login
  account, and no external notification is claimed as dispatched;
- console metadata recording is implicit; screen policy requires consent plus
  a policy reference but stores no screen/audio content;
- endpoint classification projects backup, evidence-export and telemetry
  policy without pretending that every provider executor enforces it;
- JSON/PDF evidence is classification-redacted, installation-local HMAC signed
  and persisted only as hash/signature/summary;
- organization-authored control references do not duplicate findings and do
  not imply certification;
- ransomware posture scores four bounded evidence factors and starts no
  provider mutation;
- delegated approval, operation and evidence visibility uses ten explicit
  permissions and rejects a scope bound to another provider.

B169–B175 remain `Partial` for the reasons in the feature spec: no SSO/WebAuthn
step-up, universal critical-operation integration, standalone temporary
account, external dispatcher, media recorder, public-key attestation or full
provider-native enforcement. B176–B178 remain `Done`.
