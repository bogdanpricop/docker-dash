No runtime rollback was required. The LAN activation verifier was followed by a

## Safety boundary retained

Reconciliation remains opt-in and leader-gated. Terraform support imports and
stores redacted plans but does not execute them, Ansible output is inventory
only, storage/network ownership is explicit, and signed webhook delivery
enforces timestamp, event allowlist and one-time nonce safeguards.
