**Release:** v8.61.0

## Outcome

V6.3b turns rated FinOps evidence into explainable alerts, optimization
candidates and capacity scenarios. Advisory functions start no provider action.
The optional off-hours automation path is isolated behind an explicit policy,
hash-bound approval, durable operation, typed confirmation and registered
apply/verify adapter.

## Data model and permissions

Migration 135 adds eleven stores: budget alert policies/alerts, cost anomaly
policies/anomalies, optimization assessments, savings schedules/executions,
reserved-capacity recommendations, consolidation scenarios, capacity forecasts
and placement rankings.

Three site-admin permissions are added: `finops_alerts.manage`,
`finops_optimization.manage` and `finops_capacity.manage`, bringing the
production governance catalog to 47 entries.

## Alert and detector semantics

Budget evaluation derives actual consumption against the full budget and a
daily-rate projection across the monthly/quarterly cadence. Each threshold and
signal has a stable fingerprint per rating run. Delivery channels are queue
evidence; the evaluator performs zero provider mutation.

Anomaly policies compare the current scoped amount with two to twenty-four
prior same-currency rating runs. They persist baseline run IDs, values,
direction, deviation and confidence. A zero baseline or amount below the floor
is explicitly not treated as an anomaly.

Idle and oversized assessments consume immutable ledger evidence. Low coverage,
short observation windows or missing peaks fail to insufficient-evidence states.
Critical/protected workloads activate guards. Zombie detection never deletes;
it only recommends owner review before controlled cleanup.

## Savings and capacity safety

Recommendation-mode schedules store a non-mutating execution record. Automated
start/stop requires all of the following: active `automate` policy, registered
adapter, active/succeeded durable operation, approved
`finops.schedule.power` request whose payload hash binds schedule/action/time,
and `EXECUTE SAVINGS <id>` confirmation. Success requires both adapter apply and
verify evidence.

Reservation guidance starts no purchase. Consolidation starts no evacuation or
host maintenance. Forecast starts no procurement. Placement selects no target
at the provider. Each response and audit record states the zero-side-effect
boundary.

## Control surface and verification

Governance → FinOps exposes policy creation/evaluation, per-ledger idle and
rightsizing checks, zombie review, recommendation/automatic schedules, reserved
capacity, consolidation, forecast and placement dialogs. Results are shown with
confidence, evidence hashes and mutation boundaries.

The dedicated suite covers all eleven stores and three grants; idempotent
actual/forecast alerts; baseline anomalies; idle, oversized and zombie guards;
recommendation, unsupported and fully approved schedule paths; on-prem/cloud
reservation choice; safe/blocked N+1 consolidation; growth forecast; compliant
placement ranking; and all ten advertised capabilities.
